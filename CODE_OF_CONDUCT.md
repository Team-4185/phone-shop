# Code of Conduct

## Our Standards

This project is built around professional, respectful and constructive collaboration.

Expected behavior:

- Communicate clearly and respectfully.
- Focus feedback on the work, not the person.
- Be open to different opinions, backgrounds and experience levels.
- Keep discussions relevant to the project.
- Assume good intent, but take responsibility for impact.

Unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, discrimination or personal attacks.
- Insults, threats, trolling or disruptive comments.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Repeated off-topic or hostile behavior.
- Any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional engineering environment.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, reject pull requests or block contributors who violate this Code of Conduct.

## Reporting

Report violations privately to the repository maintainers through GitHub.

Reports should include:

- What happened
- Where it happened
- Who was involved
- Any relevant links, screenshots or context

Maintainers will review reports and respond with appropriate action.
